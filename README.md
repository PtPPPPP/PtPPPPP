## 📖 About Me

I'm an **Automation Engineering** undergraduate focused on the intersection of
**perception, control, and intelligence** — building systems that can *see*, *think*,
and *act* in the real world.

My work spans from low-level embedded control up to high-level computer vision and
deep learning. I care about the full stack: how a model perceives the world, how a
controller acts on it, and how the two meet in **embodied intelligence**.

- 🤖 **Interests** — Computer Vision · Embodied AI · Robotics · AIoT · 3D Vision
- 🌱 **Currently learning** — PyTorch internals · modern YOLO · classical & modern control theory
- 🎯 **Goal** — Bridge AI research and real-world robotic / agricultural systems
- 📫 **Open to** — Internships & research opportunities in **AI / CV / Robotics / Embodied Intelligence**
- ⚡ **Fun fact** — I enjoy turning a whiteboard full of math into something that actually moves.
